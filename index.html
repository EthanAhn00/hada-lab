Human-AI & Data Analytics Lab @ HUFS

Welcome to HADA Lab.

Human-AI Data Analytics Lab.

HADA Lab explores data analytics grounded in human-AI collaboration to advance insight across knowledge-intensive domains.

What do we study?
Data Analytics, Collaborative Human-AI Systems, Knowledge-driven Insight
What analytical approaches do we use?
Text Mining, Network Analysis, Explainable AI, and Deep Learning and LLM-based Analytics
Where do we apply our research?
Technology Management, Market Intelligence, and Data & AI for Industrial Challenges
